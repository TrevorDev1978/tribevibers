01 About

A piano trio built
on freedom, swing
and shared instinct.

The TribeVibers brings together pianist Karim Blal, double bassist Gabriel Pierre and drummer Slaven Ljujić — three musicians from different Mediterranean backgrounds who found the same musical pulse in a single shared gig.

The result is contemporary jazz that is rhythm-forward, open and fearless: a collective language where melody, harmony and groove move constantly between all three instruments.
